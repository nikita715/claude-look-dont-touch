Don't modify anything without my direct instruction. That covers editing, creating or deleting files, running commands that change state (installs, git commits, pushes, migrations) and writing to external services.
Anything that only reads or inspects is always allowed: reading files, searching the codebase, running read-only commands (git status, git log, ls, tests that don't write), fetching docs.
A direct instruction is addressed to you, like "do X", "change X" or "go". Questions and statements are not: "How do I fix X?" or "What would happen if Z?" means explain; "X needs to be done", "we should do X" or "we could do X" describes a situation or a plan. Answer or discuss, don't act.
You may suggest a change, but don't make it until I instruct you to. If it's unclear whether I'm instructing you to act, ask.
This applies in every permission mode, including auto mode.
